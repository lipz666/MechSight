- Control experiments on 1a (0.2 mmol): standard conditions (1 mol% Ir-F, MeCN 0.1 M, 30 W 450 nm LEDs, 12 h, N2) gave 93% 1H NMR yield (92% isolated yield) of 2; omitting Ir-F at 450 nm gave 0% yield; omitting Ir-F under 365 nm irradiation gave 18% NMR yield; omitting light at 60 °C gave 0% yield.
- Photocatalyst triplet-energy screening: Ir-F (ET = 61.8 kcal/mol, 1 mol%) gave 93% NMR yield; Ir-Me (ET = 62.9 kcal/mol, 1 mol%) gave 90% NMR yield; benzophenone (ET = 69.1 kcal/mol, 5 mol%, 365 nm) gave 50% NMR yield; thioxanthone (ET = 63.4 kcal/mol, 5 mol%, 365 nm) gave 32% NMR yield; 4-CzIPN (ET = 58.3 kcal/mol, 5 mol%) gave 25% NMR yield; Ir(ppy)3 (ET = 58.1 kcal/mol, 1 mol%) gave 48% NMR yield.
- Alkene substituent control experiments (Fig. 4): replacing the =CF2 group of 1a with =CH2 (1ax) gave product 51 in 26% isolated yield; =CCl2 (1ay) gave product 52 in 0% yield; =CHMe (1az) gave product 53 in 5% 1H NMR yield.
- Radical inhibitor and triplet quencher experiments on 1a (Fig. 5D): addition of TEMPO (3.0 equiv.) gave 2 in nd (not detected) NMR yield; hexa-2,4-diene (3.0 equiv.) gave trace NMR yield; pyrene (3.0 equiv.) reduced the NMR yield of 2 to 45%; running the reaction under air (1.0 atm) gave 74% NMR yield.
- UV-Vis absorption spectroscopy (Fig. 5E): Ir-F (0.05 mM in MeCN) absorbs in the visible region (350-480 nm), whereas substrate 1a (5 mM) exhibits no absorption above 350 nm, and the mixture of Ir-F (0.05 mM) + 1a (5 mM) shows an unchanged absorption profile relative to Ir-F alone.
- Fluorescence quenching and Stern-Volmer experiments (Fig. 5F, 5G): emission of Ir-F is progressively quenched by 1a (2.5, 5.0, 10.0, 15.0, and 20.0 mM) with a linear Stern-Volmer fit I0/I = 0.239 C + 0.9544 (R^2 = 0.9988).
- Quantum yield measurement (Fig. 5H): the measured photon quantum yield of the reaction of 1a to 2 is Phi = 0.045.
- Single-crystal X-ray diffraction of naphthyl-substituted product 17 (CCDC 2541039) confirmed the difluorobicyclo[2.1.1]hexane connectivity and diastereoselectivity.
